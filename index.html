Our Mission

Why We Exist

"Greatness in technology is rarely loud. We exist to find it, name it, and celebrate it — so the people setting the standard get the recognition they've earned."

Rigor

Honorees are selected through a careful review of impact, craft, and sustained contribution — not popularity or self-promotion.

Breadth

From systems engineers to research scientists to product visionaries, we honor excellence wherever it appears in technology.

Legacy

Each class of honorees becomes part of a permanent record — a living archive of the people who moved the field forward.

Join Us

Apply

Become part of the community honoring excellence in technology — as a member, a judge, a speaker at our events, or an award recipient.

Member

Join a community of accomplished technologists. Attend gatherings, vote on honoree shortlists, and connect with peers at the top of their field.

Membership Criteria

  • A record of extraordinary accomplishment in technology, demonstrated through sustained, high-impact contributions to the field.
  • Service as a significant contributor to an organization of recognized standing whose work has had real impact on the technology industry and the international economy.
  • Recognition of professional distinction by peers, industry, or independent bodies.
  • A demonstrated commitment to advancing the standards and integrity of the profession.

Judge

Serve on the selection committee. Review nominations and help decide who joins the honor roll.

Judging Criteria

  • A record of extraordinary accomplishment in technology, including senior-level contributions to an organization of recognized standing with real impact on the field and the international economy.
  • Domain expertise deep enough to evaluate work at the highest levels of the profession.
  • Experience reviewing, selecting, or assessing the work of others — award panels, program committees, peer review, or equivalent.
  • A reputation for impartial, rigorous judgment and freedom from disqualifying conflicts of interest.

Speaker

Take the stage at our galas and salons. Share your work and ideas with an audience of top-tier technologists.

Speaking Criteria

  • A record of extraordinary accomplishment as a contributor to a significant organization whose work has had real impact on the technology field and the international economy.
  • Original work, insights, or perspectives that merit presentation to an audience operating at the top of the profession.
  • Demonstrated ability to communicate complex ideas clearly to expert audiences.
  • A body of work — talks, publications, products, or research — that supports the proposed topic.

Award

Be honored as an internationally recognized top contributor to your field. Award recipients receive formal recognition and join our permanent record of excellence.

Award Criteria

  • A record of extraordinary accomplishment as a contributor to a significant organization whose work has had real impact on the technology field and the international economy.
  • Contributions whose influence extends beyond a single company — recognized internationally by peers, industry, or independent bodies.
  • A sustained body of work that has advanced the state of the field, not a single isolated achievement.
  • Standing among the top contributors in the applicant's field, supported by verifiable evidence.
